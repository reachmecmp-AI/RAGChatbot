
======================================================================================================================================
1. PURPOSE
======================================================================================================================================
This policy describes paid time off (PTO), sick time, holidays, leave requests, and employee responsibilities when taking time away from work.

======================================================================================================================================
2. PTO ALLOCATION
======================================================================================================================================
2.1 Regular full-time employees accrue 15 PTO days per calendar year during their first three years of service.
2.2 Beginning with the fourth year of service, regular full-time employees accrue 20 PTO days per calendar year.
2.3 Part-time employees receive prorated PTO based on scheduled hours if eligible under their employment terms.
2.4 Temporary workers and independent contractors are not eligible for company PTO unless a written agreement states otherwise.
2.5 PTO accrual is prorated for employees who join or leave during the calendar year.
2.6 The HR system is the official record of available PTO balances.

======================================================================================================================================
3. REQUESTING VACATION PTO
======================================================================================================================================
3.1 Employees should submit planned vacation requests in the HR system at least 10 calendar days in advance.
3.2 Requests of five or more consecutive working days should normally be submitted at least 30 calendar days in advance.
3.3 Managers approve requests based on staffing, deadlines, customer coverage, and fairness across the team.
3.4 A request is not approved until the HR system shows an approved status or the manager confirms approval in writing.
3.5 Employees should not make non-refundable travel commitments before approval.
3.6 In an emergency, employees should contact their manager as soon as reasonably possible.

======================================================================================================================================
4. SICK TIME
======================================================================================================================================
4.1 Employees who are unable to work due to illness, injury, or medical appointments should notify their manager as soon as practicable, preferably before their scheduled start time.
4.2 Sick time is administered according to applicable federal, state, and local law and the employee's plan documents.
4.3 The company sample benefit provides up to 8 paid sick days per year for eligible full-time employees, subject to applicable law and any more protective local requirements.
4.4 Medical information should be shared only with HR or other authorized personnel when necessary. Managers should not request a diagnosis.
4.5 The company may request documentation only when permitted by applicable law and policy.

======================================================================================================================================
5. COMPANY HOLIDAYS
======================================================================================================================================
5.1 The company observes New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day, and Christmas Day.
5.2 HR publishes the annual holiday calendar.
5.3 If a holiday falls on a weekend, the observed date is announced by HR.
5.4 Employees required to work on a holiday should discuss scheduling and any applicable premium or alternate-day arrangements with their manager and HR.

======================================================================================================================================
6. OTHER LEAVE
======================================================================================================================================
6.1 Parental, family-care, bereavement, jury-duty, military, disability-related, and other legally protected leave are handled under applicable law and the relevant company plan.
6.2 Employees should contact HR promptly when they anticipate needing a leave of absence.
6.3 HR will explain eligibility, required forms, benefit continuation, and return-to-work steps.
6.4 This policy does not reduce rights provided by federal, state, or local law.

======================================================================================================================================
7. PTO CARRYOVER AND PAYOUT
======================================================================================================================================
7.1 The sample company permits up to 5 unused PTO days to carry into the next calendar year, unless applicable law or a written plan requires a different treatment.
7.2 PTO payout at separation is handled according to the employee's work location, applicable law, and governing plan.
7.3 Employees should review their HR-system balance before requesting time off.

======================================================================================================================================
8. MANAGER RESPONSIBILITIES
======================================================================================================================================
8.1 Managers should respond to routine PTO requests within five business days.
8.2 Managers should apply approval criteria consistently and avoid retaliation for legally protected leave.
8.3 Managers should coordinate coverage and record decisions in the HR system.

======================================================================================================================================
9. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
======================================================================================================================================
Q: How much PTO does a full-time employee receive in the first three years?
A: 15 days per calendar year, accrued during the year and prorated when applicable.

Q: How early should I request a one-week vacation?
A: Normally at least 30 calendar days before the first day off.

Q: Is my vacation approved when I submit it?
A: No. It is approved only when the HR system or manager confirms approval.

Q: What should I do if I wake up sick?
A: Notify your manager as soon as practicable, preferably before your scheduled start time, and record the absence using the HR process.

Q: Does this policy replace statutory leave rights?
A: No. Applicable law and governing benefit plans control when they provide greater rights or protections.

======================================================================================================================================
10. CONTACT
======================================================================================================================================
Contact Human Resources for leave eligibility, balances, documentation, or questions about local requirements.
