======================================================================================================================================
1. PURPOSE
======================================================================================================================================
This policy explains when employees may work remotely, how hybrid schedules are approved, and the expectations for employees working away from a company office.

======================================================================================================================================
2. POLICY SUMMARY
======================================================================================================================================
Employees may work remotely when their role can be performed effectively outside the office and their manager approves the arrangement. Remote work is not an automatic entitlement. Business needs, customer commitments, performance, information security, and applicable location-based employment requirements may affect approval.

======================================================================================================================================
3. ELIGIBILITY
======================================================================================================================================
3.1 Roles whose duties can be performed securely and effectively online may be considered.
3.2 Roles requiring on-site equipment, physical document handling, in-person customer service, or continuous facility coverage may not be eligible.
3.3 New employees may be required to work on-site during onboarding or training.
3.4 Employees must have a reliable internet connection, a suitable work area, and approved equipment.

======================================================================================================================================
4. WORK ARRANGEMENTS
======================================================================================================================================
4.1 Fully remote: The employee's approved work location is outside a company office.
4.2 Hybrid: The employee works a manager-approved combination of office and remote days.
4.3 Temporary remote work: A short-term arrangement may be approved for a defined business or personal circumstance.
4.4 The manager must document the approved arrangement, expected office days, start date, and review date.
4.5 Employees may not permanently change their work location or work from another state or country without written HR and manager approval.

======================================================================================================================================
5. SCHEDULE AND AVAILABILITY
======================================================================================================================================
5.1 Employees must follow their assigned working hours and record time accurately where required.
5.2 Employees should be reachable through approved collaboration tools during their team's core collaboration hours, normally 10:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m. local team time.
5.3 Employees should keep calendars current and notify their manager when they will be unavailable.
5.4 Remote work does not change overtime approval or timekeeping requirements for non-exempt employees.

======================================================================================================================================
6. EQUIPMENT AND EXPENSES
======================================================================================================================================
6.1 The company may provide a laptop, monitor, headset, and other equipment based on role requirements.
6.2 Company equipment remains company property and must be returned when requested or when employment ends.
6.3 Reimbursement of internet, phone, furniture, or other home-office expenses requires prior approval and must follow the Expense Reimbursement Policy.
6.4 Employees must not install unapproved software or allow household members or other unauthorized people to use company devices.

======================================================================================================================================
7. INFORMATION SECURITY AND PRIVACY
======================================================================================================================================
7.1 Employees must use company-approved devices, applications, VPN, and multi-factor authentication where required.
7.2 Confidential information must not be discussed where unauthorized people can overhear it.
7.3 Printed confidential documents should be avoided. If printing is necessary, documents must be stored securely and shredded when no longer needed.
7.4 Employees must report a lost device, suspected phishing message, accidental disclosure, or security incident to IT immediately.
7.5 Public Wi-Fi should not be used for company work unless an approved secure connection is active.

======================================================================================================================================
8. PERFORMANCE AND SAFETY
8.1 Employees are evaluated using the same performance standards regardless of work location.
8.2 Employees must maintain a safe and ergonomically appropriate workspace.
8.3 Managers may review remote-work arrangements when performance, collaboration, customer service, or business needs are not being met.

======================================================================================================================================
9. REQUEST AND APPROVAL PROCESS
======================================================================================================================================
9.1 The employee submits a written request to their manager describing the requested arrangement, work location, schedule, and proposed start date.
9.2 The manager reviews role suitability and team coverage.
9.3 HR reviews location, policy, and employment considerations when needed.
9.4 Approval must be documented in writing before the arrangement begins.
9.5 The company may modify or end an arrangement with reasonable notice, subject to applicable law and any written agreement.

======================================================================================================================================
10. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
======================================================================================================================================
Q: Can I work remotely from another state for two weeks?
A: Not without advance written approval from the manager and HR. Tax, payroll, insurance, and employment rules may apply.

Q: Does remote work change my normal working hours?
A: No. Your assigned schedule and timekeeping obligations remain in effect unless an approved schedule change is documented.

Q: Who pays for my home internet?
A: Reimbursement is not automatic. Ask your manager and follow the expense approval process before incurring an expense.

Q: Can my manager require me to come to the office?
A: Yes, subject to applicable law and any written arrangement. Business meetings, training, customer needs, or operational requirements may require on-site attendance.

======================================================================================================================================
11. INTERPRETATION
======================================================================================================================================
Questions should be directed to Human Resources. If this sample policy conflicts with applicable law or a signed employment agreement, the applicable law or agreement controls.
